How FreshEye AI works

Point a camera at your produce. Get a straight answer on whether it's still good.

FreshEye AI pairs a computer-vision model trained to recognize fruit freshness with a food-safety knowledge base, so every verdict comes with a real, cited reason — not just a label.

The end-to-end flow

What actually happens between you uploading a photo (or asking a question) and getting an answer back.

1

Create an account

A quick sign-up (name, email, password) — this is what lets your inspections and conversations be saved and found again later in your own History.

2

Upload a photo, or just ask

Use Inspect or the AI Agent page for a photo, or skip straight to Ask FreshEye if you just have a storage or food-safety question.

3

The vision model classifies it

A YOLO model trained on fresh vs. spoiled produce identifies the item and returns a label with a confidence score — in about a second.

4

Relevant knowledge is retrieved

The label is used to search a food-safety knowledge base (storage windows, spoilage signs, temperature guidance) for the passages actually relevant to that item.

5

An LLM writes the verdict

A language model combines the classification and the retrieved knowledge into a plain-language verdict, reasoning, and recommendation — grounded in real sources, not a guess.

6

Everything is saved to your account

Every chat turn on Ask FreshEye and the AI Agent is saved to your account automatically. Revisit or continue any conversation any time from History.

Two ways to get help

Both are grounded in the same knowledge base — pick based on whether you have a photo or just a question.

AI Agent

Upload once, keep asking

Upload a photo and the agent classifies it immediately, then stays in the conversation. It remembers what item you're talking about, so follow-ups like "is it still okay to use?" or "how should I store it?" just work — no need to re-explain yourself.

  • Best when you have a photo to inspect
  • Remembers the current item across the whole conversation
  • Can call tools: classify, search knowledge, generate advice
Ask FreshEye

A direct line to the knowledge base

No photo needed — just ask. Storage windows, spoilage signs, what's still safe to eat, ideal fridge temperatures. Answers are pulled straight from the same food-safety knowledge base the vision pipeline uses.

  • Best for general storage / food-safety questions
  • Fast, direct retrieval — no image required
  • Remembers recent turns in the same conversation

A typical user journey

Put together, here's what using FreshEye AI actually looks like end to end.

  1. Sign up or log in — every other page requires this, so your work is always tied to your own account.
  2. Land on Inspect or AI Agent and upload a photo of the item you're unsure about.
  3. Get an instant read — label, confidence score, and a plain-language verdict (fresh / use soon / discard) with reasoning.
  4. Ask follow-ups on the AI Agent page — "can I still cook with this?", "how long will it keep in the fridge?" — without repeating what the item was.
  5. Switch to Ask FreshEye any time for a general question unrelated to a specific photo.
  6. Revisit History later to find a past conversation and pick up exactly where you left off, on either surface.
