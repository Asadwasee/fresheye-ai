# Citrus (Oranges, Lemons, Limes)

## Freshness Indicators
Fresh citrus feels heavy for its size (a sign of juiciness), has smooth,
firm, brightly colored skin, and smells fresh and citrusy at the stem end.

## Signs of Spoilage
Soft, mushy, or wrinkled skin, visible blue-green mold patches, or a
distinctly musty/fermented smell indicate spoilage. Citrus mold (commonly
Penicillium) spreads quickly through the fruit, so any visible mold means
the whole fruit should be discarded.

## Storage Advice
Citrus keeps well at room temperature for about a week, or refrigerated in
a crisper drawer / mesh bag for 2-4 weeks. Good airflow helps prevent mold
growth from trapped moisture.
