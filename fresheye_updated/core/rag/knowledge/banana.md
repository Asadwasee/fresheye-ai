# Bananas

## Freshness Indicators
A fresh, ripe banana has bright yellow skin, is firm with a slight give,
and may have a few small brown flecks - this is normal ripening, not
spoilage.

## Signs of Spoilage
Skin that is fully black with a soft, mushy, or leaking interior, combined
with a fermented or alcohol-like smell, indicates the banana has spoiled.
Mold spots (fuzzy, not just brown/black skin) are also a spoilage sign.

## Storage Advice
Store bananas at room temperature, away from direct sunlight, and separate
from other ethylene-sensitive produce. To slow ripening once at the
desired stage, refrigerate - the skin will darken but the fruit inside
stays fine for several more days.

## If Overripe But Not Rotten
A banana with fully brown/black skin but a still-intact, non-fermented,
non-leaking interior is excellent for baking (banana bread), smoothies, or
freezing - it is not spoiled, it is simply past its best for eating raw.
