# Ideal Storage Temperature Reference

## Room-Temperature Storage (do not refrigerate until ripe)
Bananas, avocados, mangoes, papayas, tomatoes, and stone fruits (peaches,
plums, nectarines) ripen best at room temperature, roughly 18-24°C
(65-75°F), out of direct sunlight.

## Cold Storage (refrigerate immediately)
Berries, grapes, cut fruit, and leafy greens keep longest at 1-4°C
(34-39°F) in a humidity-controlled crisper drawer.

## Ripen-Then-Refrigerate
Apples, pears, kiwis, and citrus can sit at room temperature to finish
ripening, then move to the refrigerator once ripe to extend shelf life by
several days to a couple of weeks.

## Shelf-Life Ranges (approximate, for firm/fresh specimens)
Bananas: 2-7 days at room temperature. Apples: 3-4 weeks refrigerated.
Citrus: 2-4 weeks refrigerated. Berries: 3-7 days refrigerated. Tomatoes:
4-7 days at room temperature. Grapes: 1-2 weeks refrigerated.
