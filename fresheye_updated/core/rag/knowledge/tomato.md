# Tomatoes

## Freshness Indicators
A fresh tomato is firm with a slight give, has smooth taut skin, and smells
faintly sweet/earthy at the stem.

## Signs of Spoilage
Wrinkled or split skin, dark sunken soft spots, visible mold (often white
or black fuzz), and a sour, fermented, or off smell all indicate the
tomato has spoiled and should be discarded.

## Storage Advice
Store tomatoes at room temperature, stem-side down, out of direct
sunlight. Refrigeration below 13°C degrades flavor and texture even before
visible spoilage, so only refrigerate a fully ripe tomato if you need to
extend its life by a couple of days.

## If Slightly Soft But Not Moldy
A slightly soft, fully ripe tomato with no mold, no off smell, and no
sunken lesions is perfectly safe and often ideal for sauces, soups, or
salsas even if it is too soft to slice cleanly.
